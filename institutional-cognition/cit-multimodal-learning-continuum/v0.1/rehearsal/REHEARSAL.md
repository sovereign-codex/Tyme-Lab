# CIT Multimodal Learning Rehearsal v0.1

Status: bounded synthetic participant-facing rehearsal  
Authority: non-authorizing  
Deployment: local/review surface only; no public Hall deployment authorized

## Purpose

Turn the merged multimodal learning contract into one small mobile interaction surface before any public CIT projection.

The rehearsal tests a single semantic encounter across three synthetic cognitive-distance specimens while preserving the same Stewarding and Learning–Capability boundaries.

## Surface

The mobile shell exposes:

```text
TALK | SEE | TRY | MAKE | SHARE
```

It also exposes the declared assistance relationship:

```text
LEARN | DELEGATE | CO-CREATE
```

The two axes are intentionally independent. Selecting a renderer or interaction verb must not silently change assistance mode.

## Synthetic specimens

1. **Early learner** — connect the spoken phoneme /b/ to the visible letter B and an observable example.
2. **Developing builder** — understand and alter a simple repetition loop.
3. **Expert practitioner** — diagnose duplicate distributed-worker execution.

No real child data, learner identity, developmental history, credential, or institutional consequence is used.

## Interaction proof

The same session object carries:

- `semantic_state_id`;
- `encounter_mode`;
- `assistance_mode`;
- `participant_intent`;
- `active_verb`;
- `active_renderer`;
- `scaffolding_state`;
- `claim_authority`;
- `share_state`;
- append-only local evidence entries.

A renderer change may alter only `active_verb` and `active_renderer` unless an explicit governed transition changes another field.

## SHARE boundary

`SHARE` opens an offer. The participant may decline without penalty.

If accepted, the rehearsal creates only a local **Contribution Candidate** preview. It does not create a Contribution Trail, Hall Event, promoted knowledge, credential, capability grant, or public submission.

## Voice boundary

The `TALK` surface may use browser speech synthesis when available. Speech synthesis is only a renderer. Voice availability or failure does not alter the semantic state or authority posture.

No microphone capture or persistent voice recording is required for v0.1.

## Rehearsal acceptance

The validation gate requires:

- all three synthetic specimens traverse TALK -> SEE -> TRY -> MAKE -> SHARE;
- assistance mode remains explicit and participant-controlled;
- semantic state remains stable across renderer transitions;
- SHARE remains optional and pre-institutional;
- no micro-interaction emits a Hall Event;
- no global learner level, rank, score, credential, or authority escalation appears;
- child-facing specimen remains synthetic;
- a Contribution Candidate can be reconstructed from local evidence and participant reflection;
- the interface remains usable without speech synthesis.

## Stop condition

Passing this rehearsal authorizes only review of the bounded participant-facing proof. It does not authorize:

- public tymehall.org / CIT deployment;
- production participant accounts;
- real child participation;
- persistent developmental profiles;
- public Contribution intake;
- autonomous Steward authority;
- credentialing;
- schema mutation;
- Canon promotion.
