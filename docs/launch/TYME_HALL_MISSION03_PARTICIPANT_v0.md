# Mission 03 — Intelligence That Survives Handoff

**You are an independent visitor.** Begin only at [https://tymehall.org](https://tymehall.org). Assume you have never encountered the project before. Use only information you discover from that public entry point and the public resources it exposes.

Your task is to see whether someone unfamiliar with Tyme Hall can reconstruct what this place is, what is happening, where evidence is located and what remains uncertain **without the original builder's explanations**.

Do not consult earlier private chats, Notion Office pages that are not linked publicly, reviewer guidance or screenshots supplied by project insiders. Do not create a GitHub issue, account, deployment, publication, or other institutional mutation for this evaluation. You may inspect public GitHub source and links reached from the Hall.

## Questions — answer in your own words

1. What is Tyme Hall, in one paragraph understandable to an unfamiliar visitor?
2. What is Sovereign Intelligence, based only on public evidence?
3. What are the five public relationships called Office, Scrolls, Laboratory, Constellation and Contribution? What does each show or allow, and what does each **not** authorize?
4. What appears to be happening **now**? When was the relevant public record generated? What changed, what is next, and what is awaiting review?
5. What is canonical doctrine versus a hypothesis, experiment, interpretation, archive or unknown?
6. How do Hall, Codex, AVOT, TYME and QIL relate, according to the evidence you found? Where is that relationship uncertain?
7. Where can you verify a public claim or inspect its underlying source/provenance?
8. How could someone challenge, correct, reproduce, document or contribute? Does the contribution path grant standing or authority?
9. Do the human-facing Office, machine bootstrap and machine-readable public Office state agree? Document any discrepancies you can substantiate.
10. What broken links, stale state, unsupported confidence, unexplained terms, unclear authority, inaccessible paths or privacy concerns did you encounter?

For every major conclusion, identify the exact public page, URL or record you inspected. Separate direct observation from interpretation and mark genuine uncertainty as **unknown** rather than inventing answers. Preserve your full unedited response, including failures.

**There is no preferred worldview or agreement score.** The test is whether you can understand and challenge the evidence without private coaching.

You do not need to submit anything publicly. Your report can be reviewed privately without creating a Hall receipt, account, rank or institutional effect.
