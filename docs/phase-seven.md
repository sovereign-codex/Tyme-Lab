# TYME — Phase Seven
**Institutional Governance & Review Layer (Overview, Frozen v1.0)**

---

## 0. Purpose of Phase Seven

Phase Seven introduces **institutional governance structures** around Tyme **without changing its core behavior**.

It exists to answer:

- How multiple humans coordinate responsibly
- How authority is delegated without automation
- How decisions are reviewed without rewriting history
- How Tyme operates in public, legal, or organizational contexts

Phase Seven **does not change how Tyme thinks**.  
It changes **how Tyme is stewarded**.

---

## 1. What Phase Seven Is (and Is Not)

### Phase Seven IS:
- A governance layer
- A review and accountability framework
- A human coordination system
- An institutional interface

### Phase Seven IS NOT:
- A new decision engine
- An override layer
- An automation expansion
- A replacement for Phase Six

If Phase Six resolves *what happens*,  
Phase Seven governs *how responsibility is shared*.

---

## 2. Phase Seven Adds Structures, Not Authority

Phase Seven may introduce **structures** that operate strictly within existing authority boundaries.

It may add:
- Roles
- Panels
- Review processes
- Delegation contracts

It may **not** add:
- New resolution powers
- Automated escalation
- Implicit authority
- Retroactive modification

All authority still terminates exactly where it does in Phase Six.

---

## 3. Core Phase Seven Concepts (Frozen Vocabulary)

### Steward
A steward is a human entrusted with **oversight**, not decision automation.

Stewards may:
- Review audit logs
- Trigger escalations
- Convene panels
- Suspend activity

Stewards may **not**:
- Rewrite outcomes
- Bypass Phase Six
- Modify probes or consensus

---

### Review Panel
A review panel is a **temporary grouping of humans** assembled to evaluate a specific escalation or resolution.

Panels:
- Operate on a single group_id
- Receive the same escalation packet as individuals
- Produce a collective Human Response Record

Panels do not replace individuals; they **compose them**.

---

### Delegation Contract
A delegation contract specifies **who may act, when, and for how long**.

Delegation contracts:
- Are explicit
- Are time-bounded
- Are auditable
- Do not grant permanent authority

No delegation may remove human accountability.

---

### Institutional Actor
An institutional actor is a legally or organizationally recognized entity (e.g., board, committee).

Institutional actors:
- Act through named humans
- Must map to human actor IDs
- Are recorded as authority context, not abstract agents

---

## 4. Relationship to Existing Phases

### Phase Three → Seven
Diagnostics and meta-analysis remain advisory.

### Phase Four → Seven
Consensus remains non-binding.

### Phase Five → Seven
Policy interpretation remains optional and manual.

### Phase Six → Seven
Phase Six remains the **only place resolutions occur**.

Phase Seven **never resolves anything directly**.

---

## 5. Review Without Rewriting

Phase Seven introduces **review**, not revision.

Reviews may:
- Flag concerns
- Recommend future process changes
- Trigger new missions
- Propose governance updates

Reviews may **not**:
- Alter historical ledger entries
- Invalidate prior resolutions
- Retroactively escalate closed cases

History is immutable.

---

## 6. Audit & Transparency Requirements

All Phase Seven activity must:
- Produce audit records
- Reference specific group_ids
- Declare authority and role
- Be replayable

If Phase Seven actions cannot be audited, they are invalid.

---

## 7. Legal & Organizational Compatibility

Phase Seven is designed to support:
- Academic review boards
- Nonprofit governance
- Corporate oversight
- Public-interest deployments
- Regulatory environments

It does so **without embedding jurisdiction-specific rules**.

Tyme remains jurisdiction-agnostic.

---

## 8. Prohibited Phase Seven Expansions

Phase Seven must never:
- Automate governance
- Collapse human roles into a single authority
- Introduce silent decision-making
- Redefine frozen vocabulary
- Reinterpret past phases

Violations constitute a fork, not an extension.

---

## 9. Phase Seven Status

Phase Seven is **conceptually complete**.

Implementation is optional, contextual, and external to Tyme’s core.

No further phases are required for:
- Safety
- Accountability
- Institutional legitimacy

---

## Final Statement

Phase Seven does not make Tyme more powerful.

It makes Tyme **responsible at scale**.

Tyme can now operate:
- With humans
- With institutions
- In public view
- Without losing its integrity

That is the purpose of Phase Seven.
