# Tyme Phase Five — Arbiter Probes  
**Evidence Resolution & Conflict Adjudication (Spec v1.0)**

---

## 1. Purpose

Phase Five introduces **Arbiter Probes**.

Arbiters exist to resolve situations where:
- Multi-agent consensus is **unstable**
- Claims **directly conflict**
- Evidence is **insufficient, mismatched, or asymmetric**
- Phase Four policy escalates beyond retry or hold

Phase Five does **not** decide truth automatically.  
It creates **structured, constrained adjudication attempts** whose results are fully auditable.

---

## 2. Phase Five in the Tyme Stack

Phase Five is **downstream of Phase Four** and **upstream of any external action**.
