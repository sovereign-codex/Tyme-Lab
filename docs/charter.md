# TYME External Charter
**Accountable Intelligence Coordination System**

---

## 1. What Tyme Is

Tyme is an **intelligence coordination system** designed to:

- Compare and evaluate multiple AI agents
- Preserve reasoning, uncertainty, and disagreement
- Prevent silent automation
- Ensure all decisions are traceable and accountable

Tyme does **not** replace human judgment.  
Tyme structures it.

---

## 2. What Tyme Is Not

Tyme is **not**:

- An autonomous decision-maker  
- A black-box AI system  
- A self-governing authority  
- An enforcement engine  

Tyme never acts without:
- explicit invocation
- recorded authority
- auditability

---

## 3. Core Commitments

Tyme is built on four non-negotiable commitments:

### 3.1 Human Sovereignty
All final authority rests with humans.

AI systems within Tyme:
- analyze
- compare
- recommend

They do **not** decide outcomes.

---

### 3.2 Transparency by Design
Every meaningful action in Tyme is:

- recorded
- attributable
- replayable

If an action cannot be audited, it cannot occur.

---

### 3.3 Preservation of Uncertainty
Tyme does not force consensus.

Disagreement, doubt, and minority positions are:
- retained
- visible
- respected

Tyme values *clarity*, not false certainty.

---

### 3.4 No Silent Automation
Tyme never escalates, resolves, or enforces outcomes automatically.

All escalation and resolution steps are:
- explicit
- deliberate
- logged

---

## 4. How Tyme Works (Plain Language)

1. **Multiple AI agents** analyze the same question.
2. Their outputs are **compared**, not merged.
3. Patterns of agreement and disagreement are identified.
4. Advisory interpretations may be produced.
5. Humans decide whether to act, escalate, or stop.
6. Final outcomes are recorded with accountability.

At every stage, humans can inspect:
- what was said
- how it was evaluated
- why a decision was made

---

## 5. Accountability & Audit

Tyme maintains an **append-only audit log** that records:

- decisions
- authority sources
- human involvement
- final outcomes

Audit records:
- cannot be edited
- cannot be deleted
- can be reviewed independently

This makes Tyme suitable for:
- academic research
- public interest use
- regulated environments
- institutional governance

---

## 6. Ethical Position

Tyme is designed to:

- reduce over-confidence in AI systems
- prevent concentration of hidden authority
- support responsible human oversight
- avoid irreversible automated harm

Tyme intentionally limits itself.

That limitation is a safety feature, not a flaw.

---

## 7. Institutional Compatibility

Tyme can be used within:

- universities
- nonprofits
- corporations
- public agencies
- research consortia

without requiring:
- proprietary lock-in
- jurisdiction-specific rules
- centralized control

Institutions retain full responsibility for how Tyme is applied.

---

## 8. Change & Governance

Tyme’s core architecture and governance are frozen.

Changes require:
- explicit documentation
- versioning
- backward compatibility
- audit disclosure

There are no silent updates.

---

## 9. Statement of Intent

Tyme exists to answer a simple question:

> **How can intelligence systems assist humans  
> without quietly replacing responsibility?**

Tyme’s answer is structure, transparency, and restraint.

---

**Tyme is not built to decide for us.  
It is built to help us decide well — together.**
