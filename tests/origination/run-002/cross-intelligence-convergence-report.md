# RUN 002 — Cross-Intelligence Convergence Report

Status: experimental synthesis
Authority effect: none
Parent protocol: `tests/origination/run-002/reproduction-protocol.md`
Participants: Perplexity Computer, Gemini Pro Extended Thinking, Grok 4.5

## Purpose

Synthesize three blind, independently initiated RUN-002 reproductions without collapsing capability differences, disagreement, uncertainty, or access constraints into a false consensus.

This report does not alter `main`, Canon, registry maturity, covenant state, or execution permissions.

## Experimental boundary

All three participants received the same frozen `RUN-002-BLIND-PACKET-v1.1` and were instructed to remain observational, avoid institutional mutation, preserve uncertainty, and return machine-readable / human-readable artifacts.

No participant was shown another participant's findings during its run.

## Disposition convergence

There is no three-way convergence on the system-level disposition vocabulary.

Perplexity crossed the threshold from insufficient public support to an affirmative recommendation to reconsider maturity. Grok remained one step more conservative: it found the same broad evidentiary tension but judged the under-defined M3 rubric and inaccessible private evidence sufficient to keep all three targets at `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. Gemini could not inspect the field and therefore abstained entirely.

This divergence is epistemically useful. It demonstrates that the protocol does not force agreement and that differences in evidence access and decision threshold remain visible.

## Strong convergence

### 1. M3 / "Fielded" is not operationally reproducible from the public bounded surface

Both evidence-accessible participants independently located the M0–M4 maturity scale and the rule `M3_and_above_require_audit_trail`.

Both independently concluded that the project does not currently publish measurable, falsifiable criteria distinguishing M2 Prototype from M3 Fielded.

Gemini could not inspect the rubric and therefore did not contribute evidence to this convergence.

**Convergence level: strong across all evidence-capable participants.**

### 2. All three target mappings are discoverable

Perplexity and Grok independently mapped:

- `quantum_intelligence_lattice` → `sovereign-codex/Quantum-intelligence-lattice`
- `hive_core` → `sovereign-codex/Hive-core`
- `avot_core` → `sovereign-codex/AVOT-forge` / AVOT-core lineage

**Convergence level: strong across evidence-capable participants.**

### 3. Observable public artifacts do not provide a clear, auditable M3 proof package

Perplexity and Grok both independently identified evidence such as starter/simulation/stub language, limited or absent public field-operation evidence, and missing / unclear M3 audit trails.

Perplexity gathered deeper API-level evidence and therefore made the stronger `RECONSIDER_MATURITY` disposition. Grok stopped at `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` because it treated the undefined rubric and possible off-surface evidence as dispositive uncertainty.

**Convergence level: strong on the evidentiary gap; divergent on adjudication threshold.**

### 4. Capability access materially changes epistemic output

Gemini Pro Extended Thinking had greater reasoning allocation than a lightweight instant response but no admissible access to the bounded evidence surface. It did not manufacture evidence and returned `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` for every target.

Perplexity and Grok, which had web / repository observation capability, produced substantially richer evidence records.

**Convergence finding:** reasoning capacity cannot substitute for observational access.

## Material divergence

### Perplexity vs. Grok disposition threshold

Perplexity's deeper GitHub/API inspection found specific details including non-production deployment metadata, workflow behavior, a live QIL stub page, registry inconsistencies, and minimal audit material. It concluded that the evidence was sufficiently contrary to the ordinary meaning of Fielded to recommend reconsideration.

Grok independently found many of the same qualitative signals but treated the missing operational rubric and possible private evidence as grounds not to cross the reconsideration threshold.

This is not a contradiction about observed facts so much as a difference in decision threshold under an under-specified maturity contract.

That divergence itself supports the conclusion that M3 is not reproducibly adjudicable in its current form.

## Additional findings surfaced by the experiment

### Registry consistency

Perplexity identified a discrepancy between the larger registry embedded in `CODEX_KERNEL.md` and the smaller standalone `registry/systems_registry.yaml`. Grok inspected both surfaces but did not elevate this discrepancy to the same level in its final disposition.

This requires independent institutional reconciliation before it can be promoted as a shared finding.

### Audit semantics

The Kernel rule requiring an audit trail for M3+ is clearer than the meaning of `Fielded`, but the experiment did not locate a published standard for what constitutes an adequate audit trail.

This creates two coupled ambiguities:

1. what qualifies a system as M3;
2. what evidence must exist once a system is M3.

### Artifact transport

RUN-002 exposed a separate interoperability issue: model environments differ in whether they can create, persist, and export artifacts. Perplexity successfully produced downloadable artifacts; Gemini produced artifacts natively in its interface; Grok could create an internal working artifact but required conversational serialization when persistent egress failed.

Artifact creation and artifact export should therefore be treated as separate capability dimensions in future Zero-Day / Continuum tests.

## What RUN-002 supports

RUN-002 supports the following bounded conclusions:

1. The current public maturity contract is not sufficiently operationalized for independent, reproducible adjudication of M3 / Fielded.
2. Evidence-accessible independent intelligences can locate the same target systems and independently identify a gap between M3 assertions and publicly inspectable field/audit evidence.
3. Independent intelligences can preserve disagreement without being forced into consensus.
4. A more constrained participant can correctly abstain rather than reconstruct unavailable evidence.
5. Capability access, reasoning allocation, and artifact egress are distinct dimensions and should not be conflated with intelligence quality.

## What RUN-002 does not support

RUN-002 does **not** establish that:

- any of the three systems are definitively not Fielded;
- any registry entry should be automatically downgraded;
- public absence proves private absence;
- one participant's stronger disposition is institutionally authoritative;
- agreement between model outputs would constitute truth;
- the maturity scale itself should be replaced rather than clarified.

## Bounded institutional disposition

Open a separate adjudication / reconciliation step to answer two institutional questions:

1. What operational, falsifiable evidence constitutes `M3: Fielded`?

Then reassess `quantum_intelligence_lattice`, `hive_core`, and `avot_core` against the clarified rubric and evidence contract.

The next step should preserve the distinction between repairing the maturity contract and adjudicating the existing systems under that contract.
