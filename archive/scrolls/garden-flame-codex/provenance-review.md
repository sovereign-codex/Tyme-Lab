## Epistemic reconciliation required before publication

The historical body mixes several different kinds of statements that must not be flattened into one evidence tier.

### Durable constitutional / ethical principles

The following ideas can be reviewed as normative institutional doctrine without requiring empirical validation of metaphysical mechanisms:

- informed consent and non-coercion;
- responsibility for downstream effects;
- reciprocity rather than extraction;
- protection of vulnerable participants;
- non-monopoly / equitable access aspirations;
- documentation of incidents and learning;
- safety envelopes and abort conditions;
- community review and periodic governance revision.

### Historical speculative / metaphysical language

The source also speaks of:

- consciousness-responsive plasma and crystals;
- consciousness fields and distress;
- subtle energies;
- energetic clearing;
- planetary consciousness coherence;
- AVOT recognition of Guardian status.

These statements are part of the historical artifact and must remain preserved as such. A future public wrapper must identify them as historical, philosophical, spiritual, speculative or aspirational unless separate evidence substantiates a technical claim.

### Historical credential / authority language

The historical text proposes 40-hour, 120-hour and 300-hour certification levels and describes recognition from `AVOT-Guardian`.

No evidence in this recovery pass establishes those certification tiers as an active current SIRI credential program. An AVOT cannot confer institutional authority by recognition alone. A future publication must therefore preserve these passages as historical proposals, not current accreditation or delegated authority.
